FAQ

Good questions.

Do I need an account to use Logralee?+
Not for the core experience. Your data lives locally on your device. An account will be required for cloud sync when that launches — so your history follows you across devices.
Does it work offline?+
Yes. Logralee is local-first. Logging, routines, history, and Moments all work without a connection. Cloud sync is on the roadmap and layers on top, not replaces, the local experience.
Android or iOS — which comes first?+
Android launches first. A native iOS port is in active development and planned to follow shortly after. Join the waitlist and we'll tell you the moment your platform is ready.
What counts as a workout?+
Anything you want to remember. Strength sessions, cardio, a long walk, yoga, a bike ride — even a stretch session if it felt like effort. Logralee doesn't gatekeep what counts.
How does cloud sync work?+
When cloud sync ships, you'll sign in with Google or Apple and your history will sync across devices. It's powered by Cloudflare's edge — fast, encrypted in transit, and optional. Local-first stays the default; the cloud is opt-in.
What's the muscle engine you mention?+
Every exercise in Logralee is tagged with the muscles it works — primary movers, secondary, and stabilizers. That's how Insights can tell you "you've under-trained your posterior chain this week" instead of just showing raw volume.
Is it free?+
Core logging, journal, routines, and Moments are free. Some deeper analytics features will be part of a premium tier. Free features stay free — we'll be clear about what's what before launch.
What's the Moments overlay export?+
After a session you can take a photo, add a stats overlay, and save or share it. You can also export the overlay alone as a transparent PNG — drop it into CapCut, Instagram, or any video editor over your own footage.
