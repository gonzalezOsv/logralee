Email support@logralee.com. Include your phone model and what you were doing when something went wrong, and we'll get back to you.

Common questions

Do I need an account?

No. Open the app and start logging. Your journal is stored on your phone.

Does it work offline?

Yes. Everything works in airplane mode. Nothing in your journal depends on a connection.

Will my data sync between my phones, or to the web?

Not at launch. Your journal is on the device you log with. A device backup (iCloud or Google backup) carries it to a new phone.

How do I delete my data?

Delete the app. There's no copy on our side.

Is Logralee free?

Yes. Everything in the app is included at launch. If paid features come later, nothing you use today gets taken away.

Which activities can I log?

Strength, running, cycling, rowing, CrossFit, yoga, mobility and bodyweight. Strength sets, cardio, timed holds and bodyweight entries, plus your own custom exercises.

Can I make my own routines?

Yes. Build your own, or copy and edit one of the 110 ready-made routines, and pair any two so the next one is ready when you finish.

What does the coaching engine actually do?

It reads what you've logged and suggests how to train today (push it, build volume, easy day, hold steady), generates warm-up sets, suggests a lighter day when your log says you need one, and offers ranked swaps when equipment is busy. You choose how much of it you hear: Off, Minimal or Full.

Where do Moments get saved?

In the app's gallery, and, if you choose, in your Photos. Sharing to Stories or saving the see-through sticker is always optional.

Android?

Yes. iPhone and Android, both native, same features and copy.

Found a bug?

Tell us the steps to reproduce it and we'll fix it. If the app crashed, your phone may offer to send a crash report; that helps.
