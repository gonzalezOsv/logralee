Logralee in one paragraph

Logralee is a judgment-free training journal for iPhone and Android. It logs any kind of movement in seconds (lifting, running, cycling, rowing, CrossFit, yoga, mobility, bodyweight), keeps the story in a journal you'll want to scroll, and celebrates showing up without streak pressure or guilt. A coaching engine reads every set and suggests how to train in plain words; 3D guides show the movement and the muscles doing the work; Moments lays your workout over a photo. Everything is stored on the phone, with no account required. Made by Bit Ray Technologies in Romeoville, Illinois.

Facts

  • Name: Logralee, from lograr (Spanish, "to achieve") and log. Pronounced "LOG-ra-lee".
  • Platforms: iPhone (iOS 26+) and Android. Native on both.
  • Price: free to download. Everything included at launch.
  • Launch: December 2026.
  • Library: 110 routines, 26 multi-week plans, 251 exercises, 45 muscles mapped.
  • Privacy: training data stored on the device; no account; no ads; no third-party analytics.
  • Themes: seven, each in light and dark.
  • Company: Bit Ray Technologies, Romeoville, Illinois, USA.
