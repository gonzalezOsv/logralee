Privacy

Last updated: October 13, 2026

Logralee is a training journal. The short version: your journal lives on your phone. We built it that way on purpose.

What the app stores, and where

Everything you log in Logralee (workouts, sets, weights, routines, plans, body weight, Moments, settings) is stored in the app's own database on your device. It is not sent to us. There is no Logralee account to create, and no Logralee server that holds your training data.

  • Works offline. The app does not need an internet connection to log, review or edit anything.
  • Backups. Your journal is included in your phone's normal device backup (iCloud or Google backup) if you have that turned on. Those backups are governed by Apple's or Google's privacy terms, not ours.
  • Deleting your data. Deleting the app deletes your journal. There is nothing left on our side to delete.

Permissions the app asks for

  • Camera and Photos (optional): to capture a Moment and to save the finished photo or sticker to your photo library. Photos stay in the app and in your library; they are not uploaded to us.
  • Notifications (optional, when available): for reminders you choose to set.
  • Lock Screen Live Activity (iPhone): shows your current set on the Lock Screen. This stays on the device.

Sharing

When you share a Moment to Instagram, Snapchat or anywhere else, you are handing that image to that app. From there, their rules apply. Saving comes first in Logralee; sharing is always your choice.

What we don't do

  • No advertising, no ad tracking, no selling of data.
  • No third-party analytics SDKs in the app.
  • No account, no email sign-up to use the app.

Crash and diagnostic reports

If your phone is set to share diagnostics with app developers, Apple or Google may send us anonymised crash reports. These contain technical details about the crash, not your journal.

The waitlist on this website

If you join the waitlist on logralee.com, we store the email address you give us, the time you signed up, and which form you used, so we can tell you when the app launches. We also keep a short-lived, hashed record of the network address a sign-up came from, purely to block spam. We don't share waitlist emails with anyone. To be removed, email us at the address below and we'll delete it.

This website

logralee.com is a static site served by Cloudflare. It sets no cookies and runs no analytics scripts by default. Cloudflare may log requests for security and performance as described in their own privacy policy.

Children

Logralee is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from them.

Changes

If this policy changes, the date at the top changes with it. Meaningful changes will be noted in the app's release notes.
